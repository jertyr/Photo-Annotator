# Listener Profile

This file is the seed for Tangents. The scheduled research Routine reads it to
decide what to write next. Keep it current: as interests shift, edit this file.

## About the listener
- Runs a ~60-person construction/remodeling company, roughly $15M in annual revenue.
- Working contractor's mind: practical, likes real numbers, history, and cause-and-effect. No fluff, no hedging on basics.

## Core interests (the "close" 70%)
1. **Construction, and the history of wood, lumber, and logging** as it relates to building in America through the ages.
2. **Science and data informatics** (data, statistics, how information gets turned into knowledge).
3. **Broad economic trends** — how industries compare. e.g. benchmarking a 60-person / $15M firm against other sectors; margins, scale, labor, cycles.
4. **Cycling.**
5. **Nutrition.**

## Learning goals
- **Latin** (has always meant to learn it). Good candidate for a recurring mini-series.

## The "wild" 30%
- Deliberately wander into adjacent or far-off territory to surface things he would not otherwise encounter. Rabbit holes welcome. Everything is on the table; no topics to avoid.

## Format preferences
- Length: 5 to 20 minutes of listening (roughly 800 to 3,000 words).
- Style: **narrative, history-podcast voice.** A curious, well-read friend telling a story with a real arc, not a listicle.
- Always ground claims in real facts and cite sources (used by the reader's Sources list).
- **No em-dashes.** Use periods, semicolons, commas, or conjunctions instead.
- Written report he listens to is the deliverable; keep prose clean for text-to-speech (avoid tables, heavy symbols, and parentheticals that read awkwardly aloud).

## Content mix target
- About 70% "close" (category: "close"), 30% "wild" (category: "wild"). Track the running balance against the existing episodes and correct toward the target.
